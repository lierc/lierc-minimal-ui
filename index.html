IRC that stays connected when you close the tab.

Relaychat keeps your networks and channels running on our server, so you can pick up the conversation in any browser, on your laptop or your phone.

Create an account Log in

What you get

Notifications for missed mentions
Pick your highlight words, and get an email or push notification when someone mentions you while you're away.
Last-read tracking
Each channel marks where you stopped reading, on every device.
Made for phones too
A mobile layout you can add to your home screen like an app.
Searchable logs
Look back through a channel by date, sender or text.
IRCv3 reactions and typing
React to messages and see who's typing, on networks that support them.
SASL login
Sign in to networks with SASL PLAIN.
Relaychat is open source. See the project on GitHub.
